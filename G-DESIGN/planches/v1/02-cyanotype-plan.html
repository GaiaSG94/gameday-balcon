PaletteOne ink (Prussian blue) printed on paper, the way sun prints a cyanotype. Sun yellow is the single accent: light, blooms, the active plant and the score gain. Clay and leaf are low-key line colours.
TypeHumanist sans for names and the rule; a mono with tabular figures for hours, water and scores (to be embedded). Sizes 42 / 19 / 12.
MotifThe sun path is drawn in plan as an arc over the railing; each spot's floor is lit in proportion to its hours, and pot shadows are hatched away from the sun.
